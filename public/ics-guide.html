Get your calendar (.ics)

Attendance80 reads your timetable from an .ics file that you export from Zeus, EPITA's scheduling site. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open Zeus

    Go to zeus.ionis-it.com/home and sign in with your school account.

  2. Click “Generate an ICS”

  3. Choose your group and include past classes

    • Generate an ICS for a: Group
    • Search a group: type or expand the list (e.g. EPITA ▸ …) and select your class or promotion.
    • Turn on Get reservations from the past.
    • From: the first day of this semester.
    • Click Download ICS. “Generate my link” isn't needed: Attendance80 imports the file.

    Why from the start of the semester? Your attendance rate counts every class of the semester, including the ones already past. Without them, Attendance80 only sees upcoming classes and can't tell you how many absences you have left.

  4. Import it

    Open Attendance80, sign in, and drop the downloaded file on the page (or click Import .ics). Then pick your group under Courses, and mark the classes you've already been to (Sessions → Needs marking).

Good to know

  • Timetable changed? Download a fresh file the same way and import it again. Your attendance marks, tags and notes are kept.
  • The file contains every sub-group (GR1, GR2, …). Choosing yours under Courses hides the others, so they don't count toward your attendance.
  • Holidays (“Vacances”) are detected automatically and never count as missed classes.
