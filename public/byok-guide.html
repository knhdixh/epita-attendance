Use the assistant with your own free key

The assistant works right away on a shared allowance, 10 messages a day per student, and it can run out when many people use it. With your own free key it uses your own quota instead. Setup takes about 3 minutes and costs nothing.

Private and revocableYour key is stored encrypted, never shown again, and you can revoke it any time.

Cloudflare Workers AI

recommended

The same AI the shared allowance uses, but on your own free account: 10,000 "neurons" a day, about 100 messages. Cloudflare doesn't use your prompts to train models.

  1. Create a free account at dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up. No card is needed.
  2. Open AI → Workers AI and click Use REST API.
  3. Click Create a Workers AI API Token, check the details, click Create API Token, then Copy API Token. This token can only use Workers AI, nothing else in your account.
  4. On the same page, under Get Account ID, copy your Account ID (32 characters).
  5. In Attendance80, open Settings → Assistant, choose Cloudflare Workers AI, paste the Account ID and the token, and click Test & save.

When the 10,000 daily neurons run out, the assistant pauses until 00:00 UTC (02:00 in Paris in summer, 01:00 in winter). On the free plan it can't cost anything.

Groq

18+

One key, and very fast answers. The free plan allows 200,000 tokens a day, about 25 messages. Groq's contract forbids training on your prompts.

  1. Sign up at console.groq.com.
  2. Go to API Keys, click Create API Key, name it Attendance80, and copy it. It starts with gsk_ and is shown only once.
  3. In Attendance80, open Settings → Assistant, choose Groq, paste the key, and click Test & save.

Groq's terms require you to be 18 or older. Groq may keep requests for up to 30 days to check for abuse.

OpenRouter (free models)

18+

One account gives access to many models. The free ones allow 50 requests a day, about 20 messages. Free models are run by other companies under trial or research terms, so they may log or train on your prompts. Pick Cloudflare or Groq if that matters to you.

  1. Sign up at openrouter.ai. No card is needed for free models.
  2. Go to Keys, click Create Key, set the credit limit to 0 so it can never spend money, and copy the key (sk-or-…).
  3. In Attendance80, open Settings → Assistant, choose OpenRouter, paste the key, and click Test & save.

If saving fails with “No endpoints found matching your data policy”, your privacy settings block free models. Allowing them means those providers may log or train on your prompts. OpenRouter requires you to be 18 or older.

Questions

What does the app do with my key?
It sends a tiny test message when you save, then stores the key encrypted. It's only used for your own assistant messages, and it's never shown again or sent back to your browser. Remove it in Settings any time, and revoke it on the provider's website if you want to be sure.
What gets sent to the provider?
Your message, plus the context the assistant needs to answer: your courses and attendance numbers, sessions for the next few weeks, your homework and saved breaks.
It says my key was rejected.
Copy it again, with nothing extra at the start or end. For Cloudflare, check that the Account ID is the 32-character one from the Workers AI page and that the token was made with the Workers AI template.
It says I hit a rate limit or daily quota.
Your free plan's daily amount is used up. It resets the next day, or you can switch provider in Settings.
Why not Gemini, Mistral or GitHub Models?
Google's free Gemini plan isn't allowed in apps used in the EU. Mistral's free API is currently limited to business use. GitHub Models was shut down in 2026.
