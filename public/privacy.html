Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Attendance80 (epita.knhdixh.com, “the app”, “we”) helps students track class attendance, homework and breaks from their timetable. It's an independent project, not affiliated with EPITA, IONIS or Zeus. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: privacy@knhdixh.com.

Summary

  • We only collect what the app needs to work. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • Your data is stored with Cloudflare in Western Europe.
  • The AI assistant sends your question and the relevant parts of your schedule to an AI provider only when you use it.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time in Settings.

What we collect

Account
The name you enter. If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. If you use a passkey: a public key that identifies the passkey. Your fingerprint, face or PIN never leaves your device and is never sent to us.
Your timetable and attendance
The calendar (.ics) file you import (course names, times, rooms), your attendance marks, tags and notes, your group choices and settings.
Homework and breaks
Homework you add (title, notes, due date, reminder) and breaks you save. If you use the homework calendar feed, anyone with that private link can read your homework; you can reset the link in Settings at any time.
Assistant usage
How many assistant messages you send per day and how much of the AI allowance they use, to enforce fair-use limits. Conversations are kept only in your browser, not on our servers.
Your own AI key (optional)
If you add one, the API key (and Cloudflare account ID), stored encrypted. It's never shown again or sent back to your browser.
Google Calendar connection (optional)
If you connect it, an encrypted access token, the email of the connected Google account, and the ID of the “Homework (Attendance80)” calendar the app creates.
Technical data
Session cookies that keep you signed in (see Cookies). Like any website, our host Cloudflare processes your IP address and request details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

How we use it

Only to provide the app's features: showing your attendance and how many classes you can still miss, homework reminders, break planning, the assistant, calendar sync and the homework calendar feed. The legal basis is performing the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and for optional features (Google Calendar, your own AI key), your consent, which you can withdraw by disconnecting them.

The AI assistant

When you send a message, the app sends your message and the context needed to answer it to an AI model. That context is your courses and attendance numbers, sessions for the coming weeks, homework, and saved breaks.

  • Shared allowance: processed by Cloudflare Workers AI. Cloudflare doesn't use it to train models.
  • Your own key: processed by the provider you chose (Cloudflare, Groq or OpenRouter) under your account with them and their privacy terms. Some free OpenRouter models may log or train on prompts; the guide explains the differences.

The assistant only acts on your own data, and every change it makes can be undone.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, the app requests only your basic profile and email (openid email profile) to create and identify your account. If you choose to connect Google Calendar, it also requests calendar.app.created. This lets the app create one calendar, “Homework (Attendance80)”, and add, update and remove your homework events in it. The app can't see your other calendars or events.

Attendance80's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features above. It isn't sold, isn't used for advertising, isn't used to train AI models, and isn't transferred to others except as needed to provide the features or to comply with the law. Nobody reads it unless you ask for help, it's needed for security, or the law requires it. You can disconnect Google Calendar in Settings (this revokes the app's access) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who processes your data

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database (stored in Western Europe) and the shared AI assistant.
  • Google: if you sign in with Google or connect Google Calendar. The site's font is also loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads; no cookies are set.
  • Your chosen AI provider: only if you add your own key.

Some of these companies may process data outside the EU under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days, and one-time sign-in challenges after 5 minutes. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database immediately. Cloudflare's database recovery history may keep it for up to 30 days before it's overwritten.

Cookies and browser storage

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary: sid keeps you signed in, and short-lived cookies protect Google and passkey sign-in. The app also saves your assistant conversation and a few display preferences in your browser's storage. We use no advertising or analytics cookies, so there's no cookie banner.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing. You can do most of this yourself: edit or delete anything in the app, use Settings → Delete all my data for your calendar data, or Settings → Delete my account to erase everything. For anything else, email privacy@knhdixh.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. API keys and Google tokens are encrypted (AES-GCM) before they're stored. Sign-in uses passkeys or Google, so we never store passwords. Every request is checked so you can only access your own data.

Age

The app is meant for university students and isn't directed at children under 15. Some optional AI providers require you to be 18 or older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
